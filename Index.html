Welcome to Total Home Finance

This isn't just a ledger; it's a forward-looking cash flow engine. Before we build your plan, here is how the system works.

🏦

🏦 The Golden Rule: Balance = Spending Power

The "Balance" you see in this app is your actual available cash, not necessarily the literal number sitting in your checking account. It is the money you have left to safely deploy after your cash flow is calculated.

⚙️ The Four Pillars

  • 🧮 Paycheck: Where money enters the system.
  • 💸 Budget: Core bills and daily expenses.
  • ⚔️ Debt: Link Budget items directly to Liabilities. Pay the bill, drop the debt!
  • 📈 Forecast: Your financial crystal ball.

💳 The Points Game

When logging expenses, you have two choices:

  • Debit: Instantly deducts from spending power.
  • Credit: Deducts from spending power and adds to your Credit Card liability. Rack up points safely!

🎛️ Customizing Reality

  • 🛡️ Survival, ⚖️ Standard, 🚀 Dream: Toggle your dashboard to see cash flow based on bare-minimum bills, normal spending, or fully funded wishlists.
  • ⚡ Auto Pay: Mark bills so you know what handles itself.

🎁 Family Gift Cards

Track gift card balances for everyone in the family. Treat them as alternative spending power so you don't burn your actual cash!
